SETTINGS

Keybinds: click one, then press the new key (Esc cancels)

How to play

Move: WASD / left stick (keys can be changed above).
Aim + shoot: mouse / right stick.
Hold your ability key for the ability and your ultimate key for the ultimate: the further you aim, the further placed spells land. Release to cast; Esc / right-click / ✕ cancels.
Bushes hide people, houses are dark from outside.
💧 Springs heal, 🔮 wells charge your ultimate.
